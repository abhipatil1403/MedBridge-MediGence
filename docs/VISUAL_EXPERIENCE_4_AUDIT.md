## Art direction

Use a connected, offset path and bridge arches: a visual explanation of healthcare navigation, not a map of invented destinations or a clinical progress prediction. Retain deep green, ivory, sage and the official logo with visible MEDBRIDGE text. Mix confident sans-serif with editorial italic display emphasis. Give public discovery, conversation, pricing and coordination different compositions.

No legitimate provider-photo fields are present in the inspected public catalog types. Use actual names/initials and explicitly typographic institution/clinician identities. Do not invent photography, verification, outcome, availability, geography or suitability. Preserve original price precedence and included/excluded/conditional/unknown states. Existing search filters and order remain unchanged.
